# The Bandha Hotel & Suites: Brand CI

Pulled from thebandha.com, October 2026. Use this as the design system when you import `the-bandha-landing-page.html` into Claude Design.

## Logo
- Gold Balinese crown crest above the lowercase wordmark **"the bandha"**, with **HOTEL & SUITES** in small, widely spaced capitals underneath.
- File: https://cms.thebandha.com//uploads/logo_the_bandha_00a96e421c.png (565×339 PNG, transparent background)
- Colour: Bandha Gold `#826C44`. Place it only on Olive, Olive Deep or dark photography.
- Clear space: leave at least the height of the crest's top point on every side. Don't recolour, stretch or place it on a light, busy background.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Trajan Pro, the brand font on thebandha.com. Web fallback: **Cinzel** (Google Fonts) at weight 500, letter-spacing 0.04–0.08em.
- **Body:** **Open Sans Light (300)**, 16–17px, line-height 1.7, letter-spacing 0.02em.
- **Labels, eyebrows and buttons:** Open Sans SemiBold (600), uppercase, letter-spacing 0.16–0.22em, 11–13px.

## UI style
- Nearly square corners (2px radius), thin 1px gold hairlines, generous whitespace.
- Primary button: solid Bandha Gold with white uppercase text. On olive backgrounds, use a light-gold outline instead.
- Photography: warm, natural light. Lagoon pools, beachfront sunsets, neutral Balinese interiors. Lay a dark olive gradient over photos so text stays readable.

## Voice
Calm, warm and quietly luxurious, built around the line "Contemporary Balinese luxury." Keep sentences short and sensory (sunsets, lagoon, spa). Lead with value: Best Rate Guarantee, perks for booking direct.

## Conversion rules
- Every CTA reads **BOOK NOW** and links to **https://reservation.pmgbali.com/**
- USPs to repeat across the page: Best Rate Guarantee · Redeemable Room Night · 10% off F&B · 10% off Visala Spa · 9.1 Excellent guest rating · Legian beachfront · 90 rooms and suites from 47 m²
