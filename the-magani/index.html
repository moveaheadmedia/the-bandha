Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Why should I book direct instead of through a booking site?

Booking on our official site gives you our Best Rate Guarantee, member points toward room nights, 10% off dining and Visala Spa, plus exclusive offers you won't find elsewhere. Any changes are handled directly by our team.

Is breakfast included?

Our offer rates, such as 10.10 and Wine & Unwind, include a daily buffet breakfast at Mozzarella Restaurant & Bar. Room-only rates are also available, so check the inclusions when you choose your rate. Breakfast is free for kids aged 0–5.

Is The Magani good for families?

Yes. There's a shallow kids' pool, connecting Deluxe and Premium Deluxe rooms, and our Lil' Camper's Family Retreat offer with a play tent, toys and treats.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Private transfers to and from Ngurah Rai Airport take up to four passengers and cost IDR 300,000 per car for pick-up or IDR 250,000 for drop-off. Please book at least 2 days ahead. Stays of 4+ nights can include a transfer with our Airport Transfer offer.

What is the payment and cancellation policy?

Policies vary by offer. Many promotional rates need full payment when you book and are non-refundable. Each rate's terms are shown clearly before you confirm. Charges are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR).
